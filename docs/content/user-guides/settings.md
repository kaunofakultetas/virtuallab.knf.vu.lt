# Settings

The settings page contains your account details and a few personal preferences.

## Account information

You can see:

- Your username
- Your role
- Your last login time

## Changing your password

If your account has a local password, the page shows a password change form.

Requirements:

- Current password must be correct
- New password must be at least 6 characters
- New password must match the confirmation field
- New password must be different from the current one

If your account signs in through VU SSO, the page shows an info message instead of the password form.

## Appearance

Use the **Dark mode** switch to change the theme.
